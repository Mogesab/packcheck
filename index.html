BEFORE YOU BUY

A good travel item solves one problem.

  1. Check the rules. Airline size, battery, liquid, and destination rules can change.
  2. Check compatibility. Plugs, voltage, phone model, and bag measurements matter.
  3. Buy less, but better. Start with what you are missing—not a random “must-have” list.
